Founder & Owner

Name: Zaldy T. Reyes

Personal Background:

From an engineer at the MOOG company to a small business owner. The main chef in business and the founder of ZTR's first product, the chili garlic

History

Development

First began with trials and errors at home The customers back then were fellow employees at MOOG or at the owner's wife's workplace along with their friends.

Registration

Business began with the first registration for a business certificate which was later followed by an FDA certificate for the chili garlic.

DTI

Through the aid of seminars and market-matching, the business was able to improve systems of operations alongside meeting grocery owners to partenr with them as suppliers

Groceries

With the market-matching, the business moved forward to supplying products to local groceries. First, in Baguio, then, in Pangasinan, and still expands to this day.
